WISEFOXX Advisory

Commercial lease advisory for corporates, banks, and long-term investors in Pune—structured, transparent, and documentation-first.

WISEFOXX supports stakeholders through the full leasing cycle: requirement mapping, shortlisting, site visits, term-sheet alignment, negotiations, and onboarding. We focus on clarity and risk mitigation—case-specific and subject to due diligence.

Whether you are leasing Grade-A offices, transitioning to managed offices, or evaluating pre-leased bank properties, we help you compare options, align commercial terms, and move forward with confidence.

How we work

We bring structure to leasing decisions with clear comparisons, proposal alignment, and documentation support—without changing your internal approval workflows.


Advisory scope
  • Requirement mapping and micro-market selection
  • Shortlisting and proposal comparisons
  • Site visit planning and evaluation notes
  • Lease structuring: tenure, lock-in, escalation
  • Negotiation support and term-sheet alignment
  • Documentation and onboarding coordination
What you get
  • Clear comparison notes to support approvals
  • Aligned negotiation points to reduce iteration
  • Visibility into total occupancy cost drivers
  • Risk-mitigated evaluation for pre-leased assets
  • Transparent documentation support (case-specific)
  • Structured onboarding through handover
