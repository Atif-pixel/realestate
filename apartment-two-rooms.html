What to look for in a managed office

Beyond aesthetics, evaluate access, uptime, meeting-room availability, compliance, and contract terms. WISEFOXX helps ensure you compare like-for-like before signing.


Key considerations
  • Micro-market access and commute convenience
  • Inclusions: utilities, internet, housekeeping (provider-specific)
  • Meeting rooms and collaboration spaces
  • Business continuity: power backup and security
  • Contract flexibility and exit/renewal clauses
  • Branding and signage provisions (where applicable)
  • Visitor management and reception support
  • Parking options and access control
  • Clear pricing: deposits, add-ons, and escalation clauses
  • Documentation support and onboarding timelines
